Abdel · 5:30 – 6:15 · Drift monitoring

Three checks, and three different answers

data quality → hold

A zone that was busy goes silent, or hours are missing. Fix the feed, never retrain on it.

drift → retrain

PSI of pickups and of lag_1 above 0.2, against deciles of the model's own reference.

performance → retrain

Relative error (WAPE) above 1.5× the reference. Not MAE: when demand falls, MAE falls with it.

Injected on purpose

For a week, the three busiest zones report zero pickups. Not COVID: us, breaking the feed.

data_quality · hold
The trap

Forced v4 looked better on its broken holdout (0.495 vs 0.519) and judged itself "ok". On the clean week it was worse: WAPE 45.5% vs 43.8%.

Lesson

Retrain on drift, never on a broken feed. Roll back on lineage: a model trained on bad data grades itself on bad data.
